Privacy notice.

How we handle information submitted through the SPOKES website.

Website enquiries

When you complete our contact form, we collect the information you provide, including your name, organisation, work email address, property location, service interests and message. We use this information to review and respond to your enquiry, and to take steps you request before entering a potential contract.

Who receives it?

Enquiry details are transmitted to our designated business inbox and may be processed by our website hosting and email service providers. We do not publish enquiry information or sell it to third parties.

Retention and rights

We retain enquiries only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant business purpose and any applicable legal obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion where applicable under UK data protection law.

Contact

To exercise privacy rights, use the contact form and identify your request as a privacy enquiry. The organisation should review this notice and add its registered legal identity, postal address, retention periods and any required cookie information before launch.
