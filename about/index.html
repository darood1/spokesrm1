About SPOKES

Security with commercial understanding.

A considered, service-led approach to protecting workplaces, supporting building operations and helping clients manage risk.

Our approach

Built around people, premises and purpose.

Every commercial building has its own rhythm. The needs of a headquarters with high visitor volumes differ from those of a mixed-use estate, a vacant asset or a multi-tenanted office building.

We begin with the site: its layout, occupancy, operating hours, entry points, existing procedures and the expectations of tenants and visitors. That understanding informs a security solution that is relevant, proportionate and practical to deliver.

Our focus is on a professional security experience that supports the broader goals of the business. From the first impression at reception to the handling of an out-of-hours incident, small operational details matter.

How we work

A clear process from enquiry to delivery

01 / Understand

We discuss the site, its users, known concerns, coverage expectations and relevant building procedures.

02 / Design

We define a suitable service scope, responsibilities, reporting routes and the operational outcomes that matter.

03 / Review

We consider feedback, changing occupancy, incidents and evolving requirements to support continual improvement.
